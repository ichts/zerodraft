Terms

WRITE_IT_DOWN is a free browser writing trial. No account is required.

The rule is real

You write forward. Deleting, pasting, cutting, and undoing are blocked. After five seconds without input, a warning appears. After eight seconds, the current draft is deleted.

The first input starts a sixty-second session. If that deadline arrives before the wipe deadline, your text is kept on screen so you can copy it. If both deadlines arrive together, the draft is wiped. A wiped room can be restarted by typing.

No backup

Kept does not mean saved. Copy your text before leaving. The trial has no cloud storage or recovery. Do not use it as the only copy of anything you cannot afford to lose.

Your writing

Your words remain yours. We claim no ownership of them. You are responsible for what you write and how you use it.
