What comes out

Raw goes in. A draft comes back.

A zero draft is not meant to be read - it's meant to exist. Once it exists, shaping it is the easy part.

→

The shaping happens in whatever AI you already use. Copy for AI puts your raw text and a cleanup prompt on the clipboard - Zero Draft itself has no AI inside. That's the point.

House rules

  1. Forward only. Backspace, delete, paste, and undo do nothing. Mistyped a word? Keep going.
  2. Eight seconds. Pause for five and a countdown starts. Reach eight, and the draft deletes itself.
  3. Your timer. Sixty seconds is plenty for a thesis and the points around it.
