# WRITE_IT_DOWN static site

This directory is the independent writeitdown.app deployment, not the root Zero Draft site. Its approved phase-two brief chooses vanilla browser JavaScript; its look is world A in three paper modes, Lamp (default), Paper and Slate (D-119). Do not migrate the root site's Datastar runtime or bone-only palette here.
