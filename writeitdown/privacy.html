Privacy

Your draft stays in this browser tab. We do not send it to a server, save it to browser storage, or offer draft recovery.

What the browser remembers

The site stores only your light or dark appearance preference in localStorage. Clearing this site's browser data removes that preference. We do not use tracking cookies, accounts, or analytics scripts.

Requests outside your draft

The hosting server receives ordinary page requests, including your IP address and browser information. The hosting stack may keep access and security logs. Those requests do not contain your draft.

Fonts load from Google Fonts. Your browser sends Google the network information needed to request those files. See Google Fonts privacy information.

Copying and leaving

COPY TEXT writes your draft to your device clipboard only when you ask. Your operating system or clipboard manager may keep that copy. Leaving the room, reloading, or closing the tab discards the draft from this app.
