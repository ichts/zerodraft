Support

How do I start?

Choose Give it 60 seconds and type. The landing paper is a scripted preview, not the editor.

Where is my draft?

Nowhere else. Eight seconds of silence deletes it. When you finish sixty seconds, choose COPY TEXT before leaving. A wiped or closed draft cannot be recovered.

Copy did not work?

Try COPY TEXT again. If your browser blocks clipboard access, select the kept text and use your device's Copy command.
