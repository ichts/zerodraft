Buying, finding your license, and activating First Line.

Short answers to the questions that come up around the early-bird license, the Dodo license key email, and the Mac activation flow.

How do I buy First Line?

The Early Bird License is a one-time payment of USD $5. Checkout is handled by Dodo Payments as the merchant of record and opens with the early-bird release. No subscription, no recurring billing.

The purchase covers 2 Macs and includes a 14-day refund window. See the refund policy.

Where is my license key?

The receipt and license key arrive by email from Dodo Payments, sent to the address used at checkout. They are not delivered by this site.

If the email has not arrived within a few minutes, check spam, promotions, and any other inbox aliases that might have received it. Keep the email: it is your proof of purchase.

How do I activate First Line?

The Mac app gives you 3 writing sessions as a trial. When the trial ends, the app shows the Upgrade screen.

Paste the license key from the Dodo email into the license key field and activate. Activation contacts Dodo to verify the key. A valid key immediately unlocks First Line on that Mac. You can also activate from Settings.

Activation requires an internet connection. After successful activation, the app tolerates short offline periods.

How many Macs can I use?

Each license key allows 2 active Macs. Activating on a new Mac counts toward that limit. Activations are tracked by Dodo against the license key.

What if I hit the 2-Mac limit?

Self-serve deactivation is not available yet. If you need to move First Line to a new Mac beyond the 2-Mac limit, contact support at Unknown / to fill with your purchase email and license key.

My key will not activate.

Check that the key was pasted exactly as it appears in the Dodo email, with no leading or trailing spaces. Confirm that you are connected to the internet.

If the key is rejected as invalid, already at the activation limit, or refunded/revoked, contact support at Unknown / to fill with the purchase email and license key.

Refunds

Refunds are available within 14 days of purchase. See the refund policy for what to send.
