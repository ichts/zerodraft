Privacy

What First Line does, and does not do, with your writing.

First Line is built so the writing you produce stays under your control. This page describes the current behavior of the Mac app and this static website.

Your writing

Text written in the First Line Mac app stays local on your Mac. Completed sessions are saved to your Mac’s Application Support folder. Drafts are not uploaded to any server by First Line.

Drafts lost to the 8-second silence rule are not retained in any recoverable form.

Browser trial

The one-minute browser trial on the landing page runs entirely in your browser. Trial content is not sent to a server by the current static page. No analytics script is included.

Checkout and license

Checkout, tax, receipts, and license delivery are processed by Dodo Payments as the merchant of record. Dodo is the data controller for that payment data; First Line does not receive your card details.

License activation and validation requests go from the Mac app to Dodo. The request carries the license key and a generated install identifier (a short random ID stored under Application Support). It does not carry your writing.

Analytics and AI

First Line ships with no analytics, no telemetry, and no tracking in the Mac app or on this static site.

First Line does not perform on-device or remote AI processing on your writing.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact Unknown / to fill. For Dodo-specific data requests, contact Dodo Payments using the channels provided in the receipt email.
