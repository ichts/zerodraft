Questions, answered

Still unsure? Write to contact@trefoilstudio.com.

Is my writing saved or uploaded anywhere?

Nothing you write is ever saved or uploaded, because your draft stays in memory only until you copy it at the end of a session.

What happens if I pause to think while typing?

A warning appears after 5 seconds of silence and counts 3, 2, 1 before deleting your draft at 8 seconds. The Mac app uses this Standard 8-second setting by default, and it also lets you pick a Strict 5-second timer or a Relaxed 12-second timer.

Can I fix a typo or paste notes while the timer is running?

You can only type the next word during a session, because deleting, pasting, cutting, and undoing are all disabled until the timer reaches zero.

What is the difference between the free web tool and the Mac app?

The web tool gives you free 60-second sessions in your browser with no sign-up, while the Mac app offers 1, 5, 10, 20, and 30-minute sessions plus adjustable pause limits. You get 3 free sessions on the Mac app before buying a license.

Who is this tool made for?

Trefoil Studio built Write It Down for anyone who lets perfectionism stop them, whether you are tackling a first draft, a student essay, daily journaling, or NaNoWriMo.

How does the refund work if the Mac app is not right for me?

You can get a full refund within 14 days of buying the $4.99 once license with no reason needed, and you also get 3 free sessions on the Mac app to test it before paying.
