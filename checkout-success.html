After purchase

Thank you. Check your email for the license key.

Dodo Payments sends your receipt and license key by email. First Line does not use an account system, so the email is your proof of purchase and your way back in.

Next steps

  1. Open the inbox you used at checkout, including spam and promotions folders.
  2. Look for the email from Dodo Payments with your receipt and license key.
  3. Save the email. The license key is the only way to activate First Line.
  4. When the signed Mac build is released, install First Line and paste the license key when the trial ends.
