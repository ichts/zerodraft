Requirements

Requires macOS 14 Sonoma or later, on Apple silicon or Intel. The download page lists the disk image checksum and install steps.

Install

Open the disk image, drag Write It Down to Applications, then eject the disk image.

Privacy and support

The Mac app keeps writing in memory on your Mac and never uploads it. It stores settings and a local trial session counter in its settings file on the Mac. That counter is not sent anywhere. License activation and checks send license details and a random installation identifier to the license service, never your draft. See the privacy policy, the terms, and support.
