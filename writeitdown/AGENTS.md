# WRITE_IT_DOWN static site

This directory is the independent writeitdown.app deployment, not the root Zero Draft site. Its approved phase-two brief explicitly chooses vanilla browser JavaScript and both LAMPLIGHT (dark) and DAYLIGHT (light). Do not migrate the root site's Datastar runtime or bone-only palette here.

A classic script loaded before `demo.js`/`room.js`; it is in `install.sh` and must stay there.
- `status-slot.js`: the one ink placement for the warning numeral and caption shared by `room.js` and `demo.js` (numeral ink 44 px under the writing baseline, caption ink 20 px under the numeral's ink), and the placement of the stopped screen's group, whose midpoint sits at 33% of the paper height (D-070). When the warning group cannot fit above the footer it moves above the active line, the stopped group clamps above the footer, and the kept screen derives its top/bottom and compact title spacing from the same paper height; a tall sheet keeps the Mac offsets and the kept screen reserves the measured exit confirmation row at every width (inline top on desktop, wrapped row on phones), so arming it neither reflows nor overlaps a result screen.
