Write It Down for Mac

A native writing room for your Mac. Pick a session from one to thirty minutes and write forward. Deleting, pasting, cutting, and undo are blocked. Stop typing for too long and the draft is deleted. Reach the end of the clock and the text is yours to copy. Nothing is saved to disk or uploaded.

Your writing stays in memory on your Mac and is never sent anywhere. Activating and checking your license sends your license details and a random installation identifier to the license service. Your draft is never part of that request. See the privacy policy, the terms, and support.
