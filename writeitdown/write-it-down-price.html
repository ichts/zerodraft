What does Write It Down cost?

Write It Down for Mac costs $4.99 once. One license covers 2 computers, the refund window is 14 days, and there is no subscription. The web room is free.

The web room needs no sign-up and no payment step, and it does not expire. The price applies only to the Mac app, and only after its 3 free writing sessions. See the Mac page for current purchase details and license delivery.

Is Write It Down a subscription?

No. Write It Down for Mac is a one-time purchase. The price is $4.99 once. There is no renewal and no recurring charge.

How does the Write It Down refund work?

You have 14 days from purchase to ask for a Write It Down refund, and no reason is needed. The refund goes back to your original payment method, and the license stops working on every Mac.

Can I move a Write It Down license to a new Mac?

Contact Write It Down support to move a license to a new Mac. At launch, one license works on 2 computers.

Does Write It Down activation need an internet connection?

Yes. Activating and checking a Write It Down license needs an internet connection and sends license details and a random installation identifier to the license service, never your draft. The macOS privacy section covers what leaves your Mac.

Where are the current Write It Down purchase details?

How long is the Write It Down refund window?

You have 14 days from purchase to ask for a Write It Down refund. No reason is needed.

Does the Write It Down price cover the web room?

No. The Write It Down web room is free with no sign-up and no payment step. The price applies only to the Mac app.
