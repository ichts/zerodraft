Support

How do I start?

Choose Give it sixty seconds. and type. The landing paper is a scripted preview, not the editor.

Where is my draft?

Nowhere else. Eight seconds of silence deletes it. When you finish sixty seconds, choose COPY TEXT before leaving. A wiped or closed draft cannot be recovered.

Copy did not work?

Try COPY TEXT again. If your browser blocks clipboard access, select the kept text and use your device's Copy command.

Mac app

Price, download, and refunds are on the Mac page. The Mac app is not for sale yet; when it opens it will be paid. The web version here is free and complete.

License delivery and activation steps will appear here when checkout opens. At launch, one license will work on two Macs; to move it to a new Mac, contact support. A wiped or closed draft cannot be recovered. See the privacy policy for license verification details.
