What does Write It Down cost?

Write It Down for Mac costs USD 4.99 once, with no subscription, and one license covers 2 Macs with a 14-day refund.

The web room is free with no sign-up and no payment step, and it does not expire. The price applies only to the Mac app, and only after its 3 free writing sessions.

One license covers 2 Macs. You have 14 days from purchase to ask for a refund, no reason needed. The refund goes back to your original payment method, and the license stops working on every Mac. To move a license to a new Mac, contact support; at launch, one license works on two Macs.

The Mac app is not a subscription, so there is no recurring charge and no renewal. Activation needs an internet connection. Activating and checking a license sends license details and a random installation identifier to the license service, never your draft.
