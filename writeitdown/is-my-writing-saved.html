Is my writing saved in Write It Down?

Write It Down does not save your writing. The web room holds the draft in the browser tab, the Mac app holds it in memory, and neither version uploads your writing.

There is no account, cloud storage, draft history or recovery. Closing, reloading or wiping the room deletes the text, so a copy exists only if you copy the text yourself. The privacy policy covers what the site and the Mac app keep.

What does the Write It Down website store?

The Write It Down website stores one thing: your light or dark appearance preference. No draft reaches browser storage, cookies or a database. Page visits are counted without cookies and a page request never contains your writing. The web privacy policy covers storage and page visits.

How do I keep what I wrote in Write It Down?

Choose COPY TEXT when the clock ends, before you leave the Write It Down room.

Copying writes to your device clipboard only when you ask, and your operating system or clipboard manager may keep that copy. The Mac app keeps no backup either. See how Write It Down deletes a draft.

What does Write It Down keep on my Mac?

Write It Down for Mac stores settings and a local trial session counter in the app's settings file on your Mac. That counter is not sent anywhere. The Mac privacy section covers settings and network requests.

Does Write It Down upload my draft to a server?

No. Write It Down never uploads your writing. The web room keeps the draft in the browser tab and the Mac app keeps it in memory.

Does Write It Down keep a history of my drafts?

No. Write It Down keeps no draft history, because there is no account and no recovery. A draft exists only in the session that is open.

What does Write It Down for Mac send?

License activation and checks send license details and a random installation identifier to the license service, never your draft. The Mac privacy section covers the rest.
